Help & setup

Eyes Up runs a lesson from three screens: your dashboard, the classroom screen the class looks at, and the students' own devices. Here is the whole thing in three steps.

1Open the classroom screen

On the Teach page press Classroom screen. It opens in a new window: drag it onto the projector or TV and press ⛶ full screen. It shows the four-letter class code.

2Get the students in

Students go to the address on the screen, type the code and their first name. No accounts, nothing to install. Student QR puts a big code and QR on the screen; Student link copies a link for Google Classroom or Teams.

3Ask something

Pick an activity, type the question (or just say it aloud) and press ▶ Launch. Answers arrive live. 👀 EYES UP clears every screen so the class looks at you.

The screens

  • Teach page (your laptop): the Teach bar at the top with the doors to everything, Live now showing the current question and its answers, and the activity grid below. The ▴ folds the bar away on a small screen; 📌 lets an area scroll instead of staying docked.
  • Classroom screen: what the class sees. It has its own tools tab (random student, timer), a join-code tab, and buttons along the top for names, held answers, full screen and downloading the current question.
  • Teacher remote: press the door and scan the QR with your phone. Everything the dashboard does, from your pocket, so you can walk the room.
  • Student view: a live preview of exactly what students see, as an iPad held sideways.

Planning a lesson

Under Lesson plans, add activities and slides in the order you will teach them. Slides can be a PDF (PowerPoint: File → Save As → PDF) or a live Google Slides, PowerPoint Online or Canva deck. In class, ▶ Next or your presenter clicker moves through the plan one step at a time, and Room tools such as a timer or random groups can be steps too.

Common things

  • A student got logged out. They type the same code and name again and are straight back in their seat.
  • The site restarted mid-lesson. Screens reconnect by themselves and the lesson carries on with the same code.
  • Keeping the work. Every lesson is saved under ☰ Menu → Past lessons, with a printable report and a ZIP of every picture.
  • Hold answers keeps everything off the screen until you press Reveal; Names shows who said what.
