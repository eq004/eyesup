Features

One tool for the live part of a lesson: ask, see every answer, reveal, discuss. Built by a teacher, for classrooms from Kindergarten to Year 12.

⚡Over 60 activities

Fast votes, word clouds, written recall, reflection, arranging and matching, practice and tests, drawing and images, music notation, maths boards and more.

📽️A classroom screen that is yours

Full screen on the projector, student names on or off, answers held until you reveal them, a tap to spotlight one answer, and corner buttons to step through slides.

📱Teacher remote

Run the whole lesson from your phone while you walk the room. Pairs with one scan.

🙋Students need nothing

A four-letter code and a first name. Works on laptops, iPads and phones in any browser. Students who drop out rejoin the same seat.

📝Lesson plans with slides

Order activities and slides in advance, then step through with a clicker. PDF slides or live Google Slides, PowerPoint Online and Canva decks, with a question after any slide.

🧰Room tools

Timer, random student with no repeats, random groups and a question dice, on the spot or as planned steps.

✍️Letter Formation

NSW Foundation Style tracing cards with stroke order, and a replay that shows how each child formed the letter.

📚Everything kept

Past lessons with a printable report, every picture as a ZIP, a CSV of any question, and class-by-class data.

🛡️Built for a school network

Whole schools share one address, so nothing rate-limits a class. Screens reconnect by themselves after a restart.
