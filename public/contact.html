Contact

Eyes Up is made by PeakYou, a teacher-run studio in Australia. We read everything.

Contact details are being finalised. In the meantime you can reach PeakYou through peakyou.com.au.

What helps us help you

  • For a problem in class: the class code, the date and time, and what you pressed.
  • For a school licence: the school's name and roughly how many teachers.
  • For privacy questions about a child: the teacher's account name and the lesson date, and we will take it from there.
