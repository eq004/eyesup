Good to know

  • New accounts get every feature free for 30 days, then carry on with the Free plan unless they subscribe.
  • Prices are in Australian dollars. Payment is handled by Stripe; card details never touch Eyes Up.
  • A monthly subscription renews each month and a yearly one each year until you cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access to the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Your lessons and plans stay yours. If you stop subscribing, nothing is deleted.
