Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things teachers ask first.

Do my students need an account?

No. They open the address on the classroom screen, type the four-letter code and their first name. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

What devices work?

Any laptop, iPad, Chromebook or phone with a web browser. Students' screens are laid out for an iPad held sideways, and work on a phone too.

What do I need in the room?

A computer on the projector or TV for the classroom screen, and your own laptop or phone to run the lesson. They can be the same computer if they must.

A student closed the tab or got logged out. Now what?

They type the same code and the same name again and are back in their seat. Earlier answers are kept.

What happens if the site restarts during a lesson?

Every screen reconnects by itself within a few seconds and the lesson carries on with the same code. Answers already sent are kept.

Can I plan a lesson in advance?

Yes. Under Lesson plans you add activities, slides and room tools in order and teach them with ▶ Next or a presenter clicker. Plans can be reused with every class.

Can I use my PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes, two ways. Save the slides as a PDF and add them to a plan, which gives exact control over where a question lands. Or paste a link to a live Google Slides, PowerPoint Online or Canva deck, which keeps animations and video.

Where do the answers and pictures go afterwards?

Every lesson is kept under Past lessons with a printable report, a ZIP of every picture and a CSV of any question. Only you can see your lessons.

What do you store about students?

Only what they type in the lesson: a first name and their answers, including any drawings or photos they send. No student accounts, no email addresses, no tracking. See Privacy.

Is it free?

You can start free with no card. An individual subscription is $6.99 a month or $59.99 a year, and schools can take a licence. See Pricing.

Can our whole school use it?

Yes. A school licence covers every teacher on one invoice. Contact us.

Something is not working.

Refresh the page first; most things fix themselves. Then contact us with the class code and the time, and we will look at exactly what happened.
