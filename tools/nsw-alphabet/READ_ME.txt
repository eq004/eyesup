NSW FOUNDATION STYLE - TRACING ALPHABET
104 transparent PNGs, 1600 x 1600 pixels each.

Four versions of each letter:
- lowercase/dots_only
- lowercase/instructions
- uppercase/dots_only
- uppercase/instructions

The dots-only images contain only the tracing shape. Instruction images add numbered stroke starts, teal direction arrows and short movement cues. A number identifies one pen-down sequence; follow all movements in its cue without lifting. Lift before the next numbered sequence. For i and j, the final sequence adds the dot.

Every image uses the same scale and baseline. Lowercase bodies are half the height of capitals and ascenders. Descenders extend below the baseline. The tracing shape stays in the same place between paired versions. Transparent margins allow you to overlay the images on an iPad activity or your own writing lines. Use on a light background. Preserve proportions when resizing.

STYLE AND REFERENCES
These are original centre-line teaching diagrams drawn with reference to the NSW Department of Education models. They are not an official font, department-produced resource or independently certified teaching resource. The movement cues and numbered sequences are an instructional interpretation; check them against your school's chosen handwriting program before classroom rollout. Australian states use different scripts: this package is specifically NSW Foundation Style, not a national alphabet standard.

Key forms retained: single-storey a and g; looped e and k; curved lowercase v and w; straight lowercase l and uppercase I; NSW-style G, U and Y.
